# Instructions
1. Melt butter - whisk in flour
2. Slowly add milk, whisk well as you go
3. Whisk and cook until thickened
4. Add potatoes, onion, bring to boil
5. Low heat: add bacon, cheese, sour cream, salt and pepper
6. Serve! EAT!
