# Instructions

1. Place eggs in water, ensuring they are covered by ~1" of water.
2. Bring to a boil.
3. As soon as the water boils, reduce heat (if using gas) or turn off (if using electric) for one minute (optional).
4. Cover and let sit for 10-12 minutes.
5. Cool the eggs in ice water.

# Nutrition Notes

Hard-boiled eggs are an excellent, high-quality protein source. A large egg typically contains about 6g of protein and 5g of fat. These are perfect for meal prep as they keep well in the fridge for up to a week.
