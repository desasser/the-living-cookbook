# Instructions
1. In a saucepan, sauté onion, bell pepper, and celery in butter or oil until softened.
2. Add canned tomatoes (with juice).
3. Stir in Cajun seasoning, salt, and pepper.
4. Simmer on low for 20–30 minutes.
5. Serve warm.
