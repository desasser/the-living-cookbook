# Instructions

1. Fine chop the medium onion.
2. Mix all dry and wet ingredients together.
3. Ball up the mixture.
4. Heat oil until very hot.
5. Fry until golden brown.

# Nutrition Notes

Total base calories (before frying): ~1,240 calories. 

The final calorie count will depend heavily on the amount of oil absorbed during the frying process. Ensure the oil is "very hot" as specified to prevent the batter from becoming too greasy. These are best served warm immediately after frying.
